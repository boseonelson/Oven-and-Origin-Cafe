Our Story

Oven and Origin Café is a coffee, milk tea, and dessert shop established in 2024. It was built from a personal journey and a heartfelt inspiration, after the owner had her child, she was motivated to work harder and create a better future for her daughter. From that dream, the café was born.

Today, Oven and Origin Café is more than just a place to enjoy coffee. It is a warm, aesthetic, and welcoming space where customers can relax and enjoy freshly brewed coffee, milk tea, pastries, and desserts made with care. We are also proudly pet-friendly, allowing guests to bring their furry companions and share peaceful moments together in a cozy environment designed for comfort and connection.

Whether you're a coffee lover, milk tea enthusiast, busy worker, or student looking for a quiet place to study or unwind, Oven and Origin Café offers the perfect balance of quick service and calm, comfortable atmosphere. A place where stories are shared, memories are made, and every cup feels like home.
